# Company Research

## Purpose

Summarize public brand and entity signals from discovered company-facing pages.

## Scope

Home, About Us, Our Story, Farm to Fork, Contact, Certifications, Privacy Policy. No corporate registry filings.

## Methodology

Use discovery status (EV0001) plus NAP flags from template evidence (EV0011 privacy/home).

## Verified Findings

Brand presents as Namo Narayana with traditional Bilona A2 Gir cow ghee positioning. Story and contact pages return HTTP 200. Privacy content references Sonipat while site chrome references Bhiwani (**P0003**). FAQ is not a published page in `pages.json` (**FUNC0005**).
